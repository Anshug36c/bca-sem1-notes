📊 Exam Pattern — samajh lo, phir padho

Theory papers (70 marks ESE — End Semester Exam)

  • University ke external exam me 70 marks ka paper aata hai (3 hours).
  • Typical NEP pattern: Section A — short answers (2 marks × 5 ya similar), Section B — medium answers (5 marks), Section C — long answers/units se choice (10–15 marks). Exact pattern exam se pehle university circular me confirm kar lena.
  • Sessional (20) = internal tests/assignments. CIE (10) = continuous internal evaluation (quizzes, class work).

Practical papers (60 marks ESE)

  • Lab file + program write-up + viva + program execution. Aapke dono labs: Computer Fundamental Lab (MS Word/Excel + simple C programs) aur C Lab (16 listed programs).
Score-kaise-banega formula: 70-mark paper me 5 units hote hain → har unit se kam se kam 1 long question. Agar har unit ke top 6 topics tumhe aate hain, to 70 me se 55+ easy. Isliye har unit ke end wale "Most Probable Questions" section ko sabse pehle cover karna.

✅ How to Use These Notes (padhne ka sahi tarika)

  1. Pehle unit ka "Definition" box padho (📘) — yahi exam me exact likhna hai.
  2. Phir "Hinglish" box (🗣️) padho — concept dimaag me baithega.
  3. Phir "Real-life example" (🌍) — answer me example dena = extra marks.
  4. Tables zoom karo — inhi se "differentiate between" questions aate hain.
  5. Unit ke end me — Rapid Revision + Probable Questions. Question pehle khud answer karo, phir notes me check karo.
  6. Har topic ke baad apni language me 2 line likho — agar likh paaye, to yaad ho gaya.
Ek din ka perfect routine: 1 naya topic padho (40 min) → uske questions khud se likho (20 min) → pichhle topic ka revision (15 min) → C ka 1 program likho (30 min). Bas 3 mahine me syllabus khatam + 2 revision.
